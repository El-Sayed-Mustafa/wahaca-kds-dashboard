How scoring works

Scores live in the Leaderboard_Scores tab (one row per site per week) — fill it in for history, or edit a single week here and Save. Food ≥7 is green, below is red. Red ticket and Passmaster come from KDS for the selection and are coloured against their tolerances, not against the other sites — Red ticket: under 10% green, 10–15% amber, 15%+ red. Passmaster: under 15% green, 15–20% amber, 20%+ red. Each column is ranked, ranks are weighted and summed — lowest total wins. Weights are 50% Food, 25% Red ticket, 25% Passmaster, so poor food can't be offset by fast timings.
